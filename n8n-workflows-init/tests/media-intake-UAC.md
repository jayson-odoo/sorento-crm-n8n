below). Item controls: `mode:"uac"` (egress blocked, real CRM reads), `scope:"deterministic"`,
`mock_reformulator_output` (0-token parser bypass; omitted on MI-15), **`mock_media_response`** (what the CRM POST
returns — consumed by `media-extract-mock` under `media-egress-gate`), **`mock_media_poll[]`** (one body per
`GET /media/jobs/{id}` poll). Mocks mirror the CRM schemas byte-for-byte (`app/schemas/external/media.py`,
`wording.py`).

> Why the CRM call is mocked on the harness: `POST /media/process` WRITES the prod CRM (usage ledger + job row) and
> spends provider money — forbidden from the clone (CLAUDE.md safety rule). The real `media-extract-http` /
> `media-poll-http` nodes are byte-identical to what promotes and were reachability-probed with zero writes
> (`zz-media-probe` exec 13087035: `GET /media/jobs/<zero-uuid>` → 404 NOT_FOUND; `POST /media/process` with
> `modality:"video"` → 422 — proves route deployed + `crm-n8n-auth` authenticates + holds
> `integration.chatbot_media.process`; pydantic rejects before the handler, no row written).

Attachment shapes used (grounded on live execs 12924778 / 13034704): voice `{type:"audio", url, mimeType:"audio/ogg;
codecs=opus", mime, size:40000, fileName, ext}`; image `{type:"image", url, mimeType:"image/jpeg", size:153739, …}`;
pdf `{type:"file", mimeType:"application/pdf"}`. Caption = `E.text` (or `attachment.description`).
